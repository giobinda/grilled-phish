## Full description

Grilled Phish puts a red box around fake look-alike links and blocks them until you really confirm.

Scammers can copy a real website in minutes. The one thing they can't copy is its address, so they use addresses that look almost the same: paypa1.com (the number 1), pаypal.com (a Russian letter), arnazon.com ("rn" instead of "m"), paypal.com.secure-verify.xyz. Grilled Phish reads the address for you.

• Fake links get a red box and don't open when clicked. A clear warning shows where the link really goes, with the odd letters highlighted, next to the real website.
• On a fake page, a red bar appears and password and card fields are locked.
• Copies of 100,000 well-known websites are caught, not only big brands.
• Typing mistakes like githhub.com ask "Did you mean github.com?" before the site loads.
• Very new websites (most scam sites are only days old) get a milder warning.
• Strict mode, for setting it up on a parent's or grandparent's computer.
• A 5-minute guide on spotting scams.

Private by design: no account, no servers, no tracking. All checks run inside your browser. Grilled Phish will never contact you: anyone who says they are from Grilled Phish is a scammer.

Beta: Grilled Phish can miss scams, and it only checks web addresses (it is not a content filter or an antivirus). Keep your browser's own protection turned on.
